Three guarantees, three layers of truth

One identity surface for every agent. No SDK secrets. No audit trail you can't verify.

01 · AUTH

OpenID Connect, audience-bound

The resource server verifies every token against the AS's public JWKS — it holds no signing key. A token minted for /api is refused at /mcp. That's the mcp.auth.reject you watched above.

02 · REDACTION

PII never leaves the service

Every log record is redacted at creation — not at read time, not by a filter you can forget to enable. Raw identities, cards, and tokens stay inside; they never reach the agent's context window.

03 · DATABASE

The database says no

Row-level security, column grants, and a separate agent pool mean an agent's delete is refused by Postgres itself. Not middleware. rows=0 — nothing changed.
